# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Delivery times
- Amman: 1 to 2 business days.
- Other cities: 2 to 4 business days.
- Remote areas: up to 6 business days.

## Shipping fees
- Shipping costs 2 JOD inside Amman and 3 JOD for other cities.
- Shipping is free for orders of 50 JOD or more.

## Tracking
- When the order is shipped, the order shows the carrier name and a tracking number.

## Delayed orders
- If the estimated delivery date has passed by more than 2 business days, open a support ticket with the order number.
- Any compensation for a delay is decided by the support team.

## Delivery attempts
- The courier makes 2 delivery attempts. After that the order is returned to us and the customer is contacted.

## Changing the delivery address
- The address can be changed only before the order is shipped. After shipping, a change cannot be guaranteed.

## Delivery hours
- Deliveries run Saturday to Thursday, 9:00 to 18:00.
