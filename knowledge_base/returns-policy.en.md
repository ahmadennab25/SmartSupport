# Returns and Exchanges Policy

## Return window
- You can return most products within 14 days of the delivery date.
- The 14 days are counted from the day the order is marked as delivered.
- After 14 days a product cannot be returned, except for defective products covered by the warranty.

## Condition of the product
- The product must be unused, in its original packaging, with all accessories and the order number or invoice.
- Opened earphones, headphones and earbuds cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless they are defective.
- Items marked "final sale" cannot be returned.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
- Report the problem within 48 hours of delivery and attach clear photos.
- We cover the return shipping and send a replacement or refund the full amount.

## Return shipping cost
- If the return is not caused by our mistake, the customer pays a flat return shipping fee of 2 JOD, deducted from the refund.

## Exchanges
- Exchanges depend on stock availability. If the new product costs more, the customer pays the difference.

## How to start a return
- Return requests are reviewed and approved by the support team. The customer must open a support ticket with the order number and the reason.
- The AI assistant cannot approve returns or refunds by itself.
