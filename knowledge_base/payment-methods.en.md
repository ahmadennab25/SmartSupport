# Payment Methods and Payment Problems

## Accepted payment methods
- Visa and Mastercard cards, e-wallets, bank transfer and cash on delivery.

## Cash on delivery
- Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 150 JOD and has a fee of 1 JOD.
- Cash on delivery is not available in remote areas.

## Card payment failed
- Check the card limit and make sure online payments and 3D Secure are enabled, then try again.
- If it still fails, try another card or another payment method.

## Charged but the order is not confirmed
- Wait up to 24 hours: the amount may be a temporary hold that the bank releases automatically.
- If the order is still not confirmed after 24 hours, open a support ticket with the order number.

## Charged twice
- If you were charged twice for the same order, open a support ticket as urgent with the order number and the payment time. The team will review it with priority.

## Bank transfer
- Orders paid by bank transfer are confirmed within 1 business day after we receive the transfer.

## Invoices
- An invoice is issued for every order and is available on request through the support team.
