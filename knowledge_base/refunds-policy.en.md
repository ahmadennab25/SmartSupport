# Refund Policy

## When a refund is issued
- A refund is issued after the returned product is received and inspected, which takes up to 2 business days.
- Orders cancelled before shipping are refunded in full automatically.

## Refund timeline
- Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.
- If your refund has not arrived after 7 business days, open a support ticket with the order number.

## Refund method
- Card payments are refunded to the original card.
- E-wallet payments are refunded to the same wallet.
- Cash on delivery and bank transfer orders are refunded by bank transfer or as store credit, at the customer's choice.

## Partial refunds
- If accessories are missing or the product shows signs of use, the refund may be reduced after inspection.

## Shipping fees
- Shipping fees are refunded only when the problem was caused by us (damaged, defective or wrong item).

## Approval
- Refund decisions are made by the support team. The AI assistant cannot issue or promise a refund.
