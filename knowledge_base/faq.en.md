# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I track my order?
- Send your order number to the assistant. It shows the status, the carrier and the tracking number when the order has shipped.

## Can I cancel my order?
- An order can be cancelled only while it is pending, confirmed or processing, before it is shipped.
- The AI assistant cannot cancel orders. It opens a support ticket and the support team confirms the cancellation.

## Can I change my address or my order?
- Changes are possible only before shipping. Open a support ticket with the order number and the change you need.

## Is there a warranty?
- Electronics have a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects, with the invoice or order number.
- The warranty does not cover misuse, drops, water damage or unauthorized repairs.

## When can I talk to a person?
- The AI assistant is available all day. Human support agents work Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 to 17:00.
- You can ask the assistant at any time to hand your case to a human agent.

## What information should I share?
- Share only your order number and the details of the problem. Never send card numbers, card codes or passwords in the chat.
